# COBALT V3.10.6 — Opportunity intelligence precision pass

## Objective
COBALT recommends products to source from longitudinal marketplace evidence. Product identity and comparability are evaluated before demand is aggregated. The engine remains marketplace/category agnostic; automotive is the current regression corpus, not the product boundary.

## Changes
- Marketplace category path now participates directly in domain classification, with structured vehicle fields and title make+model/reference as fallbacks.
- Domain identity records confidence/evidence instead of silently assuming a vertical.
- Product parsing fixes `ignition coil` vs `ignition switch/barrel` and token-boundary model matching (`Fit` no longer matches `fits`).
- Opportunity families use precision-first representative clustering. A listing must be identity-compatible with the family representative; transitive similarity chains no longer merge unrelated products.
- Explicit product type, make/model, part-number and chassis conflicts block family membership.
- Removed the `classifyFamilyOpportunity(...) || EARLY_LEAD` fallback. A family that fails qualification creates no current Opportunity.
- Current qualification is durable state. Previously-created families that no longer qualify are retained for provenance but marked `currently_qualified=false`, moved to watching, and their unread alerts are cleared.
- EARLY leads remain in the research workspace but no longer generate bell notifications. The bell is reserved for STRONG/SOURCE NOW and strengthening events at those levels.
- Notification badge is red; notification menu adds Priority, Source now, Strong, Early and All filters. Priority orders Source now before Strong and then by recency.

## Regression expectations
- Vitz tail lights remain a coherent family.
- Colorado master window switches remain a coherent family.
- Nissan ignition coils must not be labelled ignition-with-key.
- Nissan Note wiper/headlight/window components must not reinforce one family.
- RAV4 door handles must not absorb unrelated glass/fuel-door products.
- Aqua window, combination, mirror and wiper switches must not reinforce one another merely because they share vehicle fitment.
- Failed family qualification must not default to EARLY.

## Deployment note
Run the scanner once after deployment to reconcile historical Opportunity rows against the new qualification/grouping rules.
