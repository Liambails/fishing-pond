# COBALT V3.10.12 — Close-aware adaptive cadence

New listings no longer wait six hours for the first automatic follow-up. The first successful capture schedules a 30-minute follow-up. This gives COBALT a fast second snapshot of views, bids, watchers and price without pretending that a 30-minute interval is a mature demand window.

After the initial burst, cadence expands or contracts from observed activity. Hot listings are checked more often; cold listings back off. A listing's advertised close time is also a hard scheduling cap: <=3 hours remaining -> 30 minutes, <=6h -> 1h, <=12h -> 2h, <=24h -> 4h.

If the calculated next observation would fall after auction close, COBALT instead schedules a closure confirmation for 10 minutes after the advertised close. That observation is used to confirm ended/sold state and final evidence.

The production EventBridge scheduler already wakes every 10 minutes, so 30-minute due times are actionable without changing the scheduler clock.
