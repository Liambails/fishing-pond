UPGRADING FROM V1.5.1
1. Keep both folders beside each other in Downloads:
   fishing_pond_v1_5_1
   fishing_pond_v1_5_2
2. Double-click: Migrate V1.5.1 Data.command
   This COPIES your data/history into V1.5.2 and repairs derived fields.
   It does not alter V1.5.1.
3. In Chrome -> chrome://extensions
   Remove/reload the old Fishing Pond extension and Load unpacked:
   fishing_pond_v1_5_2/chrome_extension
4. Run:
   cd ~/Downloads/fishing_pond_v1_5_2
   python3 fishing_pond.py
5. Choose 1) Start capture server.
